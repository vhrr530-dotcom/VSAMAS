Vijay Sales – Kalkaji

Every Google review scored 0–100 and sorted into Hot, Warm or Cold, so the unhappiest recent customers get a call first.

How scoring works. The CSV has no company or job data, so the rubric is adapted. Signal (max 50): Negative 50, Neutral 25, Positive 15. Recency (max 50): up to 1 month 100%, 2–3 months 75%, 4–12 months 50%, older 0%. Tiers: Hot 75+, Warm 50–74, Cold under 50. Sentiment is a keyword estimate, not a star rating. Scores within 3 points of a cut-off are marked borderline.
